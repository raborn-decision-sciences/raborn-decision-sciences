WASTELAND RAILS

Prototype v0.1 · You are the AI that runs the last supply lines.

How to play
  1. Network tab: build track segments (click a dashed line on the map, or use the list).
  2. Routes tab: "New route", then click nodes from the Hub to a city. Assign trains to routes.
  3. Trains haul cargo from the Hub. Humans pay resources + compute per delivery, scaled by how close you come to the route's expected delivery.
  4. Compute drains constantly. Run out and you go dark. Low-power mode is your emergency valve.
  5. Creature packs roam the links. Counter them with escorts (Good) or rogue turrets (Bad).
  6. Skimming: divert cargo per route. "Margin" skims only what beat expectations. Unexplained shortfalls raise suspicion; audits are announced ahead of time.
  7. Space = play/pause, N = advance to next event, S = step 1h.
